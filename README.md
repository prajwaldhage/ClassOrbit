# 🎓 Academic Operations Platform

A modern web-based Academic Management System built to simplify and automate academic operations for educational institutions.

The platform helps administrators, faculty members, and students manage academic activities such as timetable scheduling, attendance tracking, classroom allocation, and user management from a single centralized system.

> 🚧 **Project Status:** Under Active Development

---

## ✨ Features

- 🔐 Secure Authentication & Role-Based Access
- 👨‍🎓 Student Management
- 👨‍🏫 Faculty Management
- 🏫 Classroom Management
- 📅 Timetable Generation & Management
- ✅ Attendance Management
- 📊 Reports & Analytics
- 🔔 Notification System
- 📁 Academic Structure Management
- 📈 Admin, Faculty & Student Dashboards
